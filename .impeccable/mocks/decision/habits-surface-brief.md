# Habits Dashboard

Scope: Redesign the authenticated `/habits` dashboard within the existing MapaBit visual identity.

Visitor mode: Operate.

Audience: Signed-in students and young professionals checking in from desktop or a phone. Job: log today's habit quickly, then read its year-scale consistency. Primary action: log a boolean or measurable habit. Proof: the user's own entries in the 365-day heatmap. Constraints: preserve authentication, ownership, create/edit/delete, validation, and server actions; no new data or gamification.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The 365-day heatmap is the index, not decoration; each habit's daily action sits beside its history.

OWN-WORLD: Carry the landing page's near-black canvas, restrained bronze activity colors, warm white type, and selective smoked-glass framing into an operational layout. Keep Geist and familiar form controls; glass never obscures text or cells.

STORY: Scan the real totals, log today's value from its habit row, and see the matching heatmap cell confirm the entry.

FIRST VIEWPORT: A compact MapaBit header and profile actions sit above the page title and add action. Three live totals follow. The first full-width ledger rows show each habit, type, inline log control, and annual grid; month labels and a restrained activity legend orient the scan. On narrow screens, only the heatmap region scrolls horizontally.

FORM: Heatmap Ledger, the top-ranked grounded structure and Impeccable's pick, selected from the dealt hand. Its signature interaction is logging beside the annual grid without opening the edit menu. Seed key: 730f9168.
