# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Students and young professionals who want to build consistent daily habits but find existing tracking systems too complex or slow to use in the moment.

## Product Purpose

Habit Heatmap helps a signed-in user log a daily habit in under ten seconds and immediately see their consistency in a GitHub-style contribution heatmap.

## Positioning

The product pairs low-friction daily logging with a year-scale heatmap that makes gaps and consistency visible at a glance.

## Operating Context

Users access the web app to create habits, log today's entry, and review each habit's recent history. The core flow must work on mobile browsers as well as desktop.

## Capabilities and Constraints

The MVP supports email/password and social sign-in, protected user-owned habits, boolean and measurable daily logs, and one entry per habit per calendar day. The confirmed stack is Next.js App Router, TypeScript, React, Tailwind CSS, Better Auth, PostgreSQL, Prisma, and Zod. Habit entries and user-owned records must be validated and authorized server-side. Reminders, analysis, imports/exports, native apps, social features, and gamification are outside V1 scope.

## Brand Commitments

The landing page uses the name MapaBit. No logo or other brand asset is present in the project materials reviewed.

## Evidence on Hand

The app's own habit logging and heatmap are the product proof. The landing-page sample cells and habit values are illustrative; there are no supplied customer quotes, endorsements, or usage benchmarks to claim.

## Product Principles

- Keep daily logging fast and direct.
- Make consistency visible through the heatmap.
- Preserve clear ownership and privacy of habit data.
- Keep V1 focused on the personal logging and heatmap loop.
